About the Programme

Rooted in heritage.
Built for the future.

The Yoruba Political Support Group (YPSG) is a coalition committed to the political, economic, and social advancement of Yoruba communities. Through the YPSG Youth Tech Empowerment Seminar, that commitment extends into a new frontier: technology.

This seminar brings together young people, industry practitioners, and mentors for a single day of practical, honest conversation about where technology is headed — and how our youth can lead, not follow. Our goal is simple: hand every participant a clearer path into a tech-enabled future, backed by real skills, real networks, and real opportunity.

  • Practical exposure to in-demand tech fields
  • Direct mentorship from working professionals
  • A certificate that recognises your participation
